Residential Space

The Resident's Character
Becomes the Space's Language

居住者的性格成為空間的語言

Residential design starts from the person. Not a style copied onto the plan, but the resident's way of living, habits of thought and expectations of themselves — translated into the space they wake up to every day.

住家設計從人出發。不是風格的複製，而是把居住者的生活方式、思考習慣、對自己的期待——轉譯成他們每天醒來面對的空間。
