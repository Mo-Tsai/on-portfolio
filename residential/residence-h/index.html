Residence H

住家空間 · Taipei

留白不是空，是一種比例的安排，讓日常的呼吸被保留下來。

Emptiness here is not absence — it is a proportional arrangement that preserves room to breathe.

01

這個案子從衛浴開始。糞管的位置固定、動不了，馬桶一換位置，橫管就得另外找路走；走地板最省事，卻要墊高地坪，而業主不要那道高低差。於是改壁掛馬桶，橫管收進增厚的牆裡，地坪從頭到尾是平的。管線走完後牆內還剩一段深度，壁龕與鏡櫃就嵌進去——設備退到看不見的地方。公共空間取消了餐桌，讓被功能佔據的區域釋放出來。行走更鬆、停留更長、視線更安定。

This project starts in the bathroom. The soil stack could not be moved, so once the toilet shifted the horizontal run had to find another route. Along the floor would have been simplest, but it meant raising the level — and the owners did not want that step. So the toilet was hung on the wall and the run buried inside a thickened wall, leaving the floor flat from end to end. The depth still left inside that wall took the niches and the mirror cabinet — equipment retreated below visibility. In the common areas, the dining table was removed, freeing territory. Movement easier, stays longer, the eye at rest.

02

魚缸三面可視，讓玄關的第一眼先讀到透明與深度。石材牆的重點不在弧線，而在厚度怎麼被做出來——界面採凹凸層次的立體轉折，進門後的視線在這裡被導向、被緩衝。

The aquarium is visible from three sides, giving the entrance its first reading of transparency and depth. The stone wall's focus is not its curve but how thickness is achieved — layered three-dimensional folds that guide and buffer the eye upon entry.

03

牆上那片鋼板不是材質的呼應，是支撐。這裡沒有掛電視，改用雷射電視，不讓一塊黑色矩形切掉整面石材，主機就放在鋼板的檯面上。同一片鋼板，同時也是上半部整片石材的底——下層石材先固定，再加上鋼板，最後把上半部整片鋪上去。有底撐著，上半部才能維持一整面，少一道分割線。厚度不是裝飾，是結構與比例完成後自然成立的重心。

The steel plate on this wall is not a material echo; it is support. No television is mounted here — a laser TV takes its place, so no black rectangle cuts across the stone, and its unit rests on the ledge the plate forms. That same single plate is also the bed for the sheet of stone above: the lower stone was fixed first, then the plate added, then the upper stone laid across in one piece. With that bed beneath it, the upper face stays whole and carries one joint less. Thickness is not decoration; it is the center of gravity that emerges once structure and proportion are resolved.

04

一張直的吧台，四個人會並排坐成一列，各看各的。所以兩側各收一道斜邊，中間帶一段圓弧：斜邊把面向轉進來，圓弧在中間接住。檯面沒有變大，中間反而讓出空間——四個人圍成一桌，而不是四個座位。整潔來自結構性的整合——乾淨不是表面極簡，是把複雜提前整理好。

At a straight bar, four people end up seated in a row, each looking their own way. So both ends are cut back on a diagonal and the middle carries a curve: the diagonals turn people inward, the curve catches them at the center. The counter is no larger, yet the middle gives space away — four people around one table rather than four seats. Neatness comes from structural integration: cleanliness is not surface minimalism but complexity sorted in advance.

05

石材牆內的燈光，好看只是設計的一半。這塊石頭不是整面透光——光只從石英結晶透出來，底材幾乎不透，亮起來的是散在石面上的那些結晶。所以要均勻的從來不是牆的亮度，是燈的分布：分布對了，石面上才不會看見燈管的熱點。出光位置、散熱路徑、檢修動線，在結構階段一次決定。維修孔藏進分割線裡，不指出來便不會被發現。燈光為此刻而設，也為往後的年歲而設。

The lighting within the stone wall is only half about how it looks. This stone does not glow as a whole surface — light passes only through its quartz crystals, while the body of the stone stays close to opaque; what lights up is the scatter of crystals across the face. So what has to be even is never the brightness of the wall but the distribution of the lamps: get that right and no hot spot from a tube shows through the stone. Light positions, heat paths, and access for servicing were decided together at the structural stage. The maintenance panel hides within the joint lines; unless pointed out, it goes unnoticed. The light is designed for this moment, and for the years that follow.
