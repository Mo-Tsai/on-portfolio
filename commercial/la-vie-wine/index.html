La Vie Wine 樂活酒窖
Retail
零售空間
Taipei

樂活想把歐洲的酒窖帶回來。不是作為風格，而是作為一種感受——磚牆、拱頂、微光，那種只有真正走進去才能理解的沉默與重量。我們沒有去找現成的風格參考，從業主帶回來的感受出發，還原那個感受背後的空間語言。兩根不必要的柱子被放了回去——現代建築根本不需要它們，但沒有它們，空間就會說謊。牆上的壁畫借《最後的晚餐》的構圖骨架，勃根地之神坐在正中央，桌上不是麵包，是一排勃根地。當空間說的語言和品牌想說的是同一件事，那個地方就會開始有自己的引力。

What does a brand need to believe in before it builds a world? La Vie wanted to bring back a European cellar — not as a style, but as a feeling: brick walls, vaulted ceilings, dim light, the kind of silence and weight that can only be understood by walking in. We didn't look for references. We started from what the client brought back and reconstructed the spatial language behind that feeling. Two structurally unnecessary columns were put back — modern construction doesn't need them, but without them, the space would lie. The mural borrows the compositional frame of The Last Supper: the god of Burgundy sits at the center, and on the table, instead of bread, a row of Burgundy. When the space speaks the same language as the brand, the place develops its own gravity.
