敦化摩天辦公室 32F
Office
辦公空間
Taipei

臨時接手，一個月完工。這是一個從一開始就沒有「試試看」空間的案子。在有限時間裡，決策的順序完全改變——你沒辦法先做出來再改，只能先想清楚，讓工廠做完，到現場組裝。

Handed over mid-process, one month to completion. No room to try and revise — only to think clearly, then execute.

Floor Plan

所有隔屏、鐵件、櫃體全部場外預製，每一段牆體、每一處孔位，在工廠裡就確定尺寸。現場只有一件工作：精準定位，接上配件。

Every partition, every steel element, every cabinet was fabricated off-site. Every wall segment, every junction, every socket position confirmed in the factory before anything reached the site. On-site, one task only: precise positioning, connect the pieces.

時間壓力讓設計變純粹了——保留的東西，都是真正需要的；切掉的東西，都是原本可能只是「感覺不錯」的決定。

Time pressure made the design pure. What remained was what was truly needed. What was cut was what only ever felt like a good idea.
