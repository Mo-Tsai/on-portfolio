慕診所 Mu Clinic
Clinic
醫療空間
Taipei

大部分的診所，從外面就讓你準備好緊張了。玻璃招牌、冷白燈光，身體在你開門之前早已進入戒備狀態。慕診所二店從立面開始重新設計這個時刻——不是讓診所看起來比較美，而是讓走近的人，身體先鬆一點。六棵樹種在庭院平台上，森林影像輸出在玻璃格柵後面。一層是真實的綠，一層是抽象的樹林——兩個景深疊在一起，製造出一種錯覺：你不是在走進一棟建築，你是在走進一個地方。真實的植栽選擇落地種植，格柵後的森林影像刻意抽象化——放鬆不來自「像真的自然」，而來自「讓人脫離當下的城市感」。設計在這裡做的事，不是裝飾，是提前給出一個訊號：你可以放鬆了。

Most clinics prepare you to be tense before you arrive. Glass signage, cold white lighting — the body enters alert mode before the door even opens. Mu Clinic's second location redesigns that moment at the facade: not to make the clinic look more beautiful, but to let the approaching person's body relax first. Six trees are planted in the courtyard terrace; a forest image is printed behind the glass grille. One layer is real green; one layer is abstract forest — two depths overlap to create an illusion: you are not walking into a building, you are walking into a place. The plants are planted in the ground, not in pots. The printed forest is deliberately abstracted — because relaxation doesn't come from "looking like real nature," but from "allowing departure from the city." Design here is not decoration. It is an early signal: you can relax now.
