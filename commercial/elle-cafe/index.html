ELLE Café
F&B
餐飲空間
Taipei

ELLE 是一本雜誌。它習慣的語言是圖像。當它變成一個空間，它選擇放棄圖像，選擇光。

What does a media brand say when it decides to become a place? ELLE chose to speak in light, not image.

Floor Plan

白天，黑、灰、桃紅，水泥牆和金屬結構建立理性的底，光從外部滲入，空間是一本在自然光下被翻閱的雜誌。夜晚，環境暗下來，中島吧台成為唯一的發光體，桃紅光自內部透出——同一個空間，在不同光線下說兩種語言。

By day, the space is rational — concrete, steel, blush pink, natural light diffused across surfaces. By night, the ambient dims and the central bar becomes the only source of light, glowing from within. One space, two languages, no change of clothes.

品牌的個性不只存在於它說了什麼，而在於它選擇用什麼說話。

A brand's identity lives not in what it says, but in how it chooses to say it.
