耘角 Yun Jiao
Showroom
展示空間
Taipei

為一個賣材料的品牌設計展示間，是一個矛盾的問題：設計太強，材料被空間吃掉；設計太弱，沒有走進去的理由。我們選擇一件事——用空間的方式，讓塗料說話。

Designing a showroom for a material brand poses an immediate contradiction: design too strong, and the material disappears into the space; design too weak, and there's no reason to enter. We chose one thing — let the coating speak through the space itself.

Floor Plan

牆不只是背景，它是展品。立體結構讓每一道轉角都是肌理的展示，每一處反射都是塗料與光的協議。移動的時候，光跟著走。冷或暖、啞或亮，材料在不同光線下展現不同的表情。

Walls are not backgrounds; they are exhibits. Three-dimensional structures make every corner a study in texture, every surface a negotiation between material and light. As you move, the light moves with you.

設計的工作，是幫材料找到它的最佳敘述位置，然後退後一步。

The designer's role is to find the best position for the material to tell its own story, then step back.
