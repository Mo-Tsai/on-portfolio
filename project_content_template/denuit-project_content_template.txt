S4_ZH: 入口處的弧形吧台，是整個空間的第一個問答。它既是侍酒師的工作站，也是客人進入用餐狀態的過渡地帶。材質選用深色石材與拉絲銅件，在光線的映照下，讓邊界變得模糊而有層次。
S5_EN: The curved bar at the entrance is the first question and answer of the space — both the sommelier's workstation and the threshold through which guests transition into the dining experience.

[SECTION_2]

S6_ZH: 主 dining 區採用訂製半圓形卡座，將私密性與開放感並置。天花板以手工折件的金屬波浪板收尾，呼應酒窖的意象，同時創造出獨特的聲學環境，讓對話在喧囂中保有隱私。
S7_EN: The custom semi-circular banquettes place intimacy and openness side by side. The ceiling — hand-folded metal wave panels — echoes the wine cellar while creating an acoustic environment where conversation holds privacy within noise.

[SECTION_3]

S8_ZH: 燈光設計是整個空間最重要的敘事工具。暖白光源以不同角度和強度分層佈置，讓每一張桌子都擁有屬於自己的光環境，而整體氛圍仍保持一致的深沉與專注。
S9_EN: Lighting is the primary narrative instrument. Warm-white sources layered at different angles and intensities give each table its own light environment, while the overall atmosphere holds its depth and focus.
